Our story

From investment holding to automotive group

TATA Auto Trading CC, trading as Tatas Motors, is a proudly Namibian-owned automotive company headquartered in Walvis Bay. The company traces its roots to Simmecs Investments, founded in 2013, and expanded into the automotive industry in 2025 to offer complete mobility solutions across Namibia.

Today we’re a one-stop automotive solutions provider covering quality pre-owned vehicle sales, rentals, flexible financing, vehicle sourcing, special orders, imports and exports, tyres and auto parts, backed by a trusted network of local and international suppliers.

Everything we do is anchored in four values that shape how we deal with every customer who walks onto the lot or calls the showroom.
