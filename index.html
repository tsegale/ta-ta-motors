About Tatas Motors

Driving Trust.
Delivering Quality.

TATA Auto Trading CC, trading as Tatas Motors, is a proudly Namibian-owned automotive company headquartered in Walvis Bay, tracing its roots to Simmecs Investments (founded 2013) and today offering complete mobility solutions across Namibia: pre-owned vehicle sales, rentals, financing, imports and exports, tyres and auto parts.
